A small infrastructure studio in Woking

Sheerwater Consulting was founded in 2026. We build and look after the systems other work depends on: servers, clusters, pipelines and private AI.

Who leads it

Nayeem An-noor founded the studio. He has more than five years of hands-on work across Linux and Windows administration.

His work has included research computing and high-performance computing, emergency-services systems and public-sector infrastructure, and he has held UK security clearance.

He is based in Woking and works with clients across Surrey, London and the Thames Valley, in person and remotely.

Who does the work. Sheerwater is founder-led. A team of UK and offshore engineers works under his direction.

How we work

Led from the UK

Architecture, security decisions and client contact stay in the UK. A small team shares the build work so projects keep moving, and every change is reviewed before it reaches you.

Fixed scope, fixed price

You get a written scope before work starts. If something changes, we agree it in writing first.

Your work stays yours

Everything we build lives in your repositories and accounts. Contractors never hold your production credentials. Each one signs a confidentiality agreement and assigns their work to us, so we can assign it on to you.

Plain reporting

Weekly demos, a handover runbook, and honest notes on anything that did not work.
