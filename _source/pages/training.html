Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Both programmes are free and voluntary. They are training, not paid work, with no client work, and a place does not guarantee a job.

Do I get a qualification?

No accredited qualification. You get a statement of completion from Sheerwater Studios and a portfolio on GitHub.

Who can join, and can I do both?

UK-based students and early-career learners. If you are under 18, we ask for a parent or guardian's consent. The Infrastructure programme needs no experience; AI Engineering Foundations needs basic Python. Each takes about 7 to 8 hours a week, so most people do one at a time.
