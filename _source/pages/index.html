How a project runs

  1. Scoping call

    Thirty minutes, free. If we are not the right people for the job, we will say so.

  2. Written scope

    One page: what we build, what we do not, how long it takes and the price.

  3. Build

    Weekly demos. Everything goes into your own repositories, so you keep the work.

  4. Handover

    A runbook and a walkthrough for your team. Support afterwards is optional.
