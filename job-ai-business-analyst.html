About Sheerwater Consulting

Sheerwater is a founder-led UK infrastructure and AI consultancy based in Woking, Surrey. We sell fixed-scope services including on-premises infrastructure, Azure and AWS landing zones, Kubernetes, and private AI that runs on a client's own systems. We are early stage and building our first client work.

About the role

The AI Business Analyst helps us decide which problems are worth solving with private AI, and shapes how we scope, test and report on each pilot. It is a voluntary advisory role for someone with business analysis experience who wants to work on AI projects alongside a small team. You choose what to take on and when.

What you could work on

  • Discovery: build a standard discovery questionnaire and requirements template for our services, starting with the private AI pilot
  • Use-case selection: help a client find one task where AI will save time, and define what a good result looks like
  • Pilot testing: plan and run testing sessions with a client's team and write a short verdict report
  • Data and risk review: map where a client's data sits and what must stay in-house
  • Proposals: turn discovery notes into a one-page proposal and statement of work
  • Training programme: help keep our student programme organised
  • Hands-on AI: build a prompt library and test our local AI demo, using Claude and other tools

Who this suits

  • Experience in business analysis: stakeholder interviews, requirements, process mapping and documentation
  • Interest in AI, and ideally some project experience with chatbots or similar tools
  • Clear writing, and confidence talking to non-technical people
  • Reliable communication: you tell us when you are busy, and we do not chase
  • No deep technical background needed

What you get

  • Real AI project experience for your CV and LinkedIn, with a title and a short written reference where earned
  • Practice with Claude and other AI tools on real business tasks
  • A say in how our private AI offer is shaped

How it works

  • Part time, on and off. There are no set hours, no minimum commitment and no deadlines we can enforce.
  • Either side can stop at any time, without notice or reason.
  • This is unpaid and is not employment. It creates no contract of employment, and we make no promise of future paid work.
  • Client information stays confidential, and we ask you to confirm this in writing.

How to apply

  1. Apply

    Share your LinkedIn profile. GitHub is optional. No CV needed.

  2. We reply

    We read every application ourselves and reply by email.
