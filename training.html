Our training team runs two free, part-time, remote programmes for UK students. Each lasts 12 weeks, with one live evening a week and a hands-on project you push to GitHub.

Infrastructure Engineer AI Engineering Foundations

Infrastructure Engineer Training Programme

Twelve weeks that take you from the Linux command line to running containers and machine-learning workloads on Kubernetes. Each week has one live evening of about 2.5 hours, then a project you build in your own time.

What you learn

  • Git, GitHub and the Linux command line, then Linux administration: users, SSH, systemd, networking and storage
  • Proxmox virtualisation, with a VMware comparison
  • Ansible for patching, hardening and repeatable builds
  • Docker, Kubernetes and an introduction to MLOps
  • A capstone project to finish

AI Engineering Foundations

Twelve weeks on building AI applications that run on your own hardware. You start by running a first local model and finish with a deployed project, with a defined project every week.

What you learn

  • Python for data, machine-learning basics and PyTorch
  • How language models work, prompting and running models locally
  • Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG): searching your own documents, with citations and testing
  • Fine-tuning a small model with LoRA
  • AI agents that use tools and ask for approval first
  • Deploying with Docker and an API, plus responsible AI, then a capstone showcase

How both programmes work

  1. Live evening

    One session a week of about 2.5 hours: show and tell, a taught topic with a live demo, then the start of that week's project.

  2. Weekly project

    You finish it in your own time and push it to a public GitHub repository with a README.

  3. Feedback

    Written feedback on your projects, and support from the training team between sessions.

  4. Portfolio

    You finish with a set of working projects and a capstone you can show to employers.

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Both programmes are free and voluntary. They are training, not paid work, and they do not involve client work. Nothing is promised afterwards, and a place does not guarantee a job.

Do I get a qualification?

No accredited qualification. If you complete the programme you get a statement of completion from Sheerwater Consulting and a portfolio of projects on GitHub.

Who can join?

UK-based students and early-career learners. The Infrastructure programme needs no prior experience. AI Engineering Foundations needs basic Python.

Can I do both?

You can apply for both, but each takes about 7 to 8 hours a week, so most people do one at a time.
