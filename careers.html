How hiring works

The process is short, and you are paid for any work you do for us, including the trial.

  1. Apply

    Fill in a short form with links to your work. It takes about five minutes.

  2. Intro call

    Thirty minutes about your experience, the kind of work you want and your availability.

  3. Paid trial task

    A small, real task with a written brief, paid at your agreed rate, so both sides see how it goes.

  4. Contract

    A written agreement covering the work, the rate, confidentiality and ownership of what you build.

Project Manager
Keep our remote engineering interns organised, on schedule and talking clearly with the UK.
  • TypeContract
  • LocationFully remote
  • SalaryNegotiable

About the role

You are the day-to-day link between the founder in the UK and our team of engineering interns. You turn written scopes into plans, keep everyone unblocked and make sure clients get honest, regular updates. You do not write production code.

What you would work on

  • Break each project scope into weekly plans, tasks and milestones
  • Run a short daily stand-up and a weekly demo with the UK
  • Track progress, risks and blockers, and raise them early
  • Write clear weekly status reports that the founder can share with clients
  • Match interns to tasks and help new starters settle in
  • Keep the project board, meeting notes and handover documents up to date

Requirements

  • A degree in any subject; a business, IT or engineering degree is a plus
  • At least two years coordinating software, IT or similar project delivery
  • Good fluency in written and spoken English, as most communication is in English
  • Confident using a task board such as Jira, Trello or Linear
  • A working understanding of agile ways of working (sprints, stand-ups, retrospectives)
  • Reliable, organised and calm when plans change
  • Able to give feedback kindly and clearly, and to say when something is at risk

Nice to have

  • A project management qualification such as PRINCE2, PMP or a Scrum certificate
  • Experience supervising students or junior staff
  • Basic understanding of cloud, DevOps or web development

How we assess

A paid task: turn a short sample scope into a two-week plan with risks and a status report. Every trial task is paid.

DevOps and Platform Engineer Intern
Learn to build cloud, Kubernetes and automation for real client infrastructure projects.
  • TypeInternship
  • LocationFully remote
  • SalaryNegotiable

About the role

Work alongside an experienced technical lead and the founder to build the infrastructure that other software runs on. You will start with small, well-defined tasks and take on more as you grow. Everything you build is reviewed before it reaches a client.

What you would work on

  • Write Terraform and Ansible to set up cloud and Linux servers
  • Help build and harden Kubernetes clusters
  • Create CI/CD pipelines that test and deploy software automatically
  • Set up monitoring with tools such as Prometheus and Grafana
  • Document every build in a runbook that clients can follow themselves
  • Take part in weekly demos and explain what you built

Requirements

  • Minimum: a completed bachelor's degree (or final-year student finishing within six months) in computer science, IT, engineering or a related field
  • Good fluency in written and spoken English
  • Working knowledge of Linux on the command line
  • Basic scripting in Bash or Python
  • Understanding of Git and version control
  • Basic knowledge of networking: IP addresses, DNS, firewalls, SSH
  • Careful habits with passwords, keys and changes to live systems
  • Willing to explore new, fun challenges and to learn by building, breaking and fixing things
  • Curious, patient and honest about what you do not yet know

Nice to have

  • A home lab, virtual machines or personal projects you can show us
  • Experience with Docker, Terraform, Ansible, Azure or AWS
  • Progress towards a certification such as Azure Fundamentals, CKA or Terraform Associate

How we assess

A paid task: deploy a small app to Kubernetes from a Git repository, with an automatic pipeline. Every trial task is paid.

  • Good fluency in written and spoken English
  • Solid Python, including working with libraries such as NumPy or pandas
  • Understanding of the basics of machine learning and how large language models work
  • Comfortable using Linux and the command line
  • Understanding of Git and version control
  • A habit of testing claims and checking results before reporting them
  • Willing to explore new, fun challenges, including problems nobody has solved before
  • Respect for data privacy and confidentiality

Nice to have

  • Experience with tools such as Ollama, vLLM, LangChain or vector databases
  • Familiarity with Docker and GPUs
  • A thesis, project or portfolio involving machine learning
