How hiring works

The process is short, and you are paid for any work you do for us, including the trial.

  1. Apply

    Fill in a short form with links to your work. It takes about five minutes.

  2. Intro call

    Thirty minutes about your experience, the kind of work you want and your availability.

  3. Paid trial task

    A small, real task with a written brief, paid, so both sides see how it goes.

  4. Contract

    A written agreement covering the work, the salary, confidentiality and ownership of what you build.

Terms for everyone

  • Salary is negotiable, agreed up front and paid on time
