Services and fixed-price packages

Each package has a stated deliverable and timeline, and a fixed price agreed in writing before work starts. Choose the closest one and we adjust the scope to fit you.

For research groups

Research groups usually have capable people, fixed funding and no spare engineer. These packages focus on getting compute, storage and software environments working for scientists, and on leaving a setup your own team can run once we have gone.

For startups

Startups need to move quickly without building up infrastructure debt. These packages give you a repeatable base, faster releases and a clear handover, so your first engineer is not stuck untangling hand-built servers.

For regulated businesses

Law, health and finance teams carry duties around client and patient data. These packages put access control, audit trails and documented data flows first. We are clear about what we build and what remains your responsibility, including compliance itself.

For IT providers

IT providers need dependable extra capacity for client work. These packages are scoped as repeatable engagements. We work as an extension of your team and hand over documentation your own engineers can pick up and reuse.
