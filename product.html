What we are open to building

If your idea fits one of these, we want to hear it. If it sits somewhere in between, describe it and we will say plainly whether we can do it well.

  • Mobile apps

    iOS and Android apps for a business, a community or a new idea: from a first working version to a launch-ready app.

  • Websites for businesses

    Clear, fast websites for small businesses and organisations, with forms, bookings or a simple content system where you need them.

  • Games

    Browser and mobile games, from small prototypes to full releases.

Where the team is today

The product team is being built. It is led by our engineering side and is growing with the projects it takes on, so we keep the number of builds small enough to do them properly.

We are also working on our own products, starting with private AI that runs on a customer's own infrastructure. We only build a product of our own once real buyers have told us they need it.

How a build runs

  1. Idea call

    Thirty minutes, free. You describe what you want and who it is for.

  2. Written scope

    What we build first, what waits for later, how long it takes and the price.

  3. Build in small steps

    You see a working version regularly, so you can change direction early.

  4. Launch and handover

    The code goes in your own repository. Ongoing support is optional.
