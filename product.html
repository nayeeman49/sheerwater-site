Mobile apps

Scoped and priced per project

Apps for phones and tablets, for a business, a community or a new idea, from a first working version to a launch-ready release.

A typical first release

We agree the one thing the app must do well, design the key screens, build it in short stages with a test version on your own phone each week, then prepare the store listings.

What we can build

  • Customer apps with accounts, profiles and push notifications
  • Booking, ordering and loyalty apps for a local or online business
  • Community and events apps
  • Apps that work offline and sync when connected
  • Payments and sign-in with the usual providers

Good fit for

Founders and organisations with a clear idea who want a first version in users' hands quickly.

What we need from you

A sketch or a short description of what the app does, who uses it and anything it must connect to.

Small businesses, practices and organisations that need a professional site without a long project.

AI features and assistants

Scoped and priced per project

Add AI to an app or a website: answer questions from your own documents, with sources, and ask a person before anything important is done.

A typical first release

We pick one task, test it on real examples from your work, and show you how often the answers are right before it is built into the product.

What we can build

  • Chat assistants that answer from your documents and show the source
  • Summaries and drafts inside an existing app
  • Approval steps before an AI sends or changes anything
  • Assistants that run on your own servers when data must stay private
  • Testing so you can see how often answers are right

Good fit for

Teams that want AI inside a product or a workflow without handing control to it.

What we need from you

The task, a few real examples, and where the data is allowed to go.

Where the team is today

The product team is in progress. It is being built alongside the projects it takes on, so we keep the number of builds small enough to do them properly.

We are also working on our own products, starting with private AI that runs on a customer's own infrastructure. We only build a product of our own once real buyers have told us they need it.

How a build runs

  1. Idea call

    Thirty minutes, free. You describe what you want and who it is for.

  2. Written scope

    What we build first, what waits for later, how long it takes and the price.

  3. Build in small steps

    You see a working version regularly, so you can change direction early.

  4. Launch and handover

    The code goes in your own repository. Ongoing support is optional.
