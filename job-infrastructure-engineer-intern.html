About Sheerwater Studios

Sheerwater is a UK infrastructure and AI consultancy based in Woking, Surrey. We design and build on-prem, cloud and local AI infrastructure for research groups, startups and small organisations, using Linux, Proxmox, VMware, Ansible, Kubernetes and Terraform.

About the programme

This is a structured, part-time, fully remote 3-month training internship, not a job in the usual sense. Each week has one live evening session of about 2.5 hours, then a project you build in your own time and push to GitHub. There are 30 places, with monthly 1-to-1 check-ins and written feedback on every project. It is voluntary and unpaid, it does not involve client work, and it does not lead to an accredited qualification. You finish with a GitHub portfolio, a statement of completion and a clear record of what you built.

Dates

The first session is on Wednesday 16 December 2026. There are no sessions on 23 and 30 December, then weekly sessions resume on Wednesday 6 January 2027. The final showcase is on Wednesday 17 March 2027.

What you'll learn

Weeks 1 to 7: foundations

  • Git, GitHub and the Linux command line (weeks 1 to 3)
  • Linux administration: users, permissions, SSH, systemd, networking, storage and Bash scripting
  • Proxmox: virtual machines, templates, networking and backups, with a VMware comparison (weeks 4 to 5)
  • Ansible: inventory, playbooks, roles, patching and hardening (weeks 6 to 7)

Weeks 8 to 12: modern platforms

  • Docker: images, containers, volumes and Compose (week 8)
  • Kubernetes: pods, deployments, services, ingress, configuration and monitoring (weeks 9 to 10)
  • MLOps: serve and monitor a small local AI model (week 11)
  • Capstone showcase evening and feedback (week 12)

What you'll do

  • Build and harden a Linux server, and write a backup and health-check script
  • Build a Proxmox lab, back up and restore a virtual machine, and write a short migration plan
  • Automate setup and patching across several servers with Ansible
  • Containerise an app and deploy it to a Kubernetes cluster
  • Run a small local AI model behind an API with simple monitoring
  • Document every project so someone else can follow it

What you'll get

  • A live session every week, monthly 1-to-1 check-ins and written feedback on every project
  • A GitHub portfolio of about 10 small projects and one capstone
  • Project descriptions for your CV and LinkedIn
  • A statement of completion from Sheerwater Studios, and a reference from the founder on successful completion, where earned

Who this suits

  • Studying or recently graduated in computing, IT or engineering, or teaching yourself
  • Curious about servers, Linux and automation. No prior experience required.
  • Able to commit around 8 hours a week for 3 months, with breaks for exams
  • A laptop with 16 GB RAM, or willingness to use a shared lab host
  • If you are under 18, we ask for a parent or guardian's consent
