UI and UX Designer Intern

Design clear, accessible interfaces for mobile and web products.

  • TypeInternship
  • LocationFully remote
  • SalaryNegotiable
Apply for this role

About the role

Design the screens and flows people actually use. You will work with engineers and the founder from sketch to finished interface, and see your designs built and shipped.

What you would work on

  • Turn short briefs into wireframes, flows and polished screens in Figma
  • Design mobile and web interfaces that are clear and accessible
  • Build and maintain small design systems of colours, type and components
  • Prepare designs and notes that developers can build from
  • Review the finished build against the design
  • Test designs with simple user feedback and improve them

Requirements

  • Minimum: a completed bachelor's degree (or final-year student finishing within six months) in design, HCI, computer science, media or a related field, or an equivalent portfolio
  • Good fluency in written and spoken English
  • A portfolio of at least two projects; student and personal work counts
  • Skill in Figma or a similar design tool
  • Understanding of layout, typography, colour and accessibility basics
  • Able to explain your design choices clearly
  • Willing to explore new, fun challenges and take feedback well
  • Interest in how products are built and how real people use them

Nice to have

  • Basic HTML and CSS
  • Experience with prototyping or user testing
  • Mobile app design experience

How we assess

A paid task: redesign one screen from a short brief and explain your choices. Every trial task is paid.

How to apply

  1. Send the form

    About five minutes. Include links to your work, such as GitHub, a portfolio or an online CV.

  2. Intro call

    Thirty minutes about your experience and availability.

  3. Paid trial task

    A small, real task with a written brief. You are paid for it.

  4. Offer

    A written agreement covering the work, salary, confidentiality and ownership of what you build.
