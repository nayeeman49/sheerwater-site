How a project runs

  1. Scoping call

    Thirty minutes, free. We listen, ask questions and tell you if we are not the right fit.

  2. Written scope and price

    One page covering what is built, what is not, the timeline and a fixed price.

  3. Build in short stages

    Weekly demos and everything in your own repositories, so you can see progress and keep the work.

  4. Handover

    A runbook and a walkthrough for your team. Ongoing support is optional.
